hfm-player

Media player with AI-based filtering for haram content

Filters human figures from video and music from audio using local AI models. Runs entirely offline - no cloud, no tracking. Portable - no installation required.

Video filter (human segmentation) Audio filter (music removal) GPU acceleration (DirectML / OpenVINO) Portable (single folder)
